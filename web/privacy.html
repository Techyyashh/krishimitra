KrishiMitra ("the app") is a student project that helps farmers identify crop diseases from leaf photos. This policy explains what we collect, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Leaf photos: processed in memory only to make a prediction. We do not store your photos.
  • Anonymous scan records: the predicted disease name, confidence score and time. No name, phone number, location or IP address is stored with it.
  • Feedback (optional): whether the result was correct and any comment you type. Please do not include personal details in comments.
  • Questions and advice requests: the text of your question and the detected disease are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate an answer. They are not used to identify you.
  • Voice: advice text is sent to a text-to-speech service (Google Translate TTS via gTTS) to create audio.
  • On your phone only: your language choice and recent scan history are saved in your browser and never leave your device. Clearing browser data deletes them.

Why we collect it

To give you a result and advice, to improve the accuracy of the AI model, and to count how the app is used.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data for advertising. Data is processed only by the service providers listed above and our hosting provider.

Retention and your rights

Anonymous scan and feedback records are kept for up to 24 months. You can ask questions or request deletion of any feedback you sent by writing to the contact email above.

Children

The app is meant for farmers and is not directed at children under 18.

Terms of Use

  • KrishiMitra gives general, AI-generated guidance. It can be wrong. It is not a substitute for advice from an agricultural expert.
  • Before using any pesticide or chemical, confirm with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or agriculture officer and always follow the dose and safety instructions on the product label.
  • The app is provided "as is", free of charge, without any warranty. The developers are not responsible for crop loss, damage or injury resulting from use of the app.
  • Do not misuse the service (for example, automated mass requests).
