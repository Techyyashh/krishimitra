# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background, language or experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, or sharing of anyone's private information.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**. All reports are handled confidentially.
